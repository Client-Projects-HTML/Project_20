Our Story

Two Painters, One Van, and a Promise

ColorCraft began in 2011 when our founder and one apprentice took on a single interior repaint with a borrowed ladder and a promise: leave every home cleaner than we found it. Word spread through referrals long before we ran a single ad.

Fifteen years later we're a full crew of certified painters and finishers, but the promise hasn't changed — every wall gets treated like it's in our own home.

Our Mission

Deliver flawless, on-time finishing work with transparent pricing, so every client knows exactly what they're paying for and exactly when it'll be done.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted painting and wall-finishing company in the region — known as much for our site discipline as our color work.
