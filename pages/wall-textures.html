Service Overview

Create Stunning Designer Walls with Premium Texture Finishes

Our decorative wall texture service transforms ordinary walls into beautiful design features. From subtle modern finishes to luxurious marble effects and rustic textures, we create elegant walls that add personality and depth to every room.

Using premium texture materials and professional application techniques, we deliver durable, stylish finishes suitable for living rooms, bedrooms, offices, restaurants, hotels, and commercial interiors.

Starting at ₹45 / sq.ft

What's Included

  • Surface inspection and wall preparation
  • Crack filling and smooth base coating
  • Decorative texture application
  • Protective finishing coat
  • Complete cleaning after installation

Materials Used

We use premium decorative texture products from Asian Paints, Berger, Dulux and Nerolac. Choose from rustic, stucco, sandstone, metallic, marble, concrete, floral and designer finishes available in matte, satin and luxury metallic effects.

Estimated Time

Most feature walls can be completed within 1–2 days, while larger designer texture projects generally require 3–5 days depending on the texture style and drying time.
