Service Overview

Rust Protection & Durable Metal Finishes

Protect your gates, window grills, balcony railings, and metal structures from corrosion and weathering. We provide comprehensive metal preparation including rust scraping, zinc-phosphate priming, and high-gloss enamel or spray-applied Polyurethane coatings.

Starting at ₹25 / sq.ft

Request a Quote for This Service

What's Included

  • Wire brushing & mechanical rust scraping
  • Degreasing & surface sanding
  • Anti-corrosive red oxide / zinc chromate primer
  • Two coats of synthetic enamel or PU metal paint
  • Surrounding area masking & drop-cloth protection

Materials Used

Asian Paints Apcolite Premium Enamel, Berger Luxol, Dulux Gloss, or MRF Metal Coat — weather-resistant, UV-stable formulations designed to resist chipping and rusting.

Estimated Time

1–2 days for standard home window grills and railings; 3–4 days for large main entrance gates and compound fencing.
