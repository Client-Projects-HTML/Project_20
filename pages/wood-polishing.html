Service Overview

Restore Grain, Enhance Elegance

Enhance and protect your wooden fixtures with our expert polishing and refinishing services. From traditional French polishing to high-durability Polyurethane (PU) spray coatings, we bring out the rich grain and luster of your doors, trim, staircases, and custom furniture.

Starting at ₹45 / sq.ft

Request a Quote for This Service

What's Included

  • Old finish stripping & deep wood sanding
  • Wood filler application for dents and gouges
  • Custom grain staining (Walnut, Teak, Rosewood, Natural)
  • Multi-coat sealer & topcoat (Matte, Gloss, or Satin)
  • Buffing & final protective clear coat

Materials Used

Sayerlack, ICA PU finishes, Asian Paints WoodTech, or MRF Melamine — non-yellowing, scratch-resistant lacquers and stains engineered to protect against humidity, heat, and everyday wear.

Estimated Time

2–3 days for main doors and trim; 3–5 days for dining sets or full room woodwork, accounting for proper drying time between coat applications.
