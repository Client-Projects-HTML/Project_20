Service Overview

Room-Ready Finishes, Zero Disruption

Our interior painting service covers everything from a single accent wall to a full-home repaint. We protect your furniture and flooring, patch and sand every surface, and apply two coats of your chosen finish for even, lasting color.

Starting at ₹18 / sq.ft

Request a Quote for This Service

What's Included

  • Furniture & floor covering
  • Crack filling & surface sanding
  • Primer + two finish coats
  • Edge & trim detailing
  • Full site cleanup on completion

Materials Used

Asian Paints Royale, Berger Silk, or Dulux Velvet Touch — low-VOC, washable finishes in matte, satin, or sheen. Premium tier includes stain-blocking primer for water-marked walls.

Estimated Time

1–2 days per standard room (10×12 ft); 4–7 days for a full 3BHK apartment, including drying time between coats.
