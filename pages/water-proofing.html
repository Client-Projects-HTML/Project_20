Service Overview

Advanced Seepage & Leak Protection

Our professional waterproofing service eliminates wall dampness, peeling paint, roof leaks, and structural erosion. We use advanced moisture meters, high-grade elastomeric coatings, and specialized crack fillers to permanently shield terraces, bathrooms, and exterior walls.

Starting at ₹35 / sq.ft

What's Included

  • Thermal & moisture-level site inspection
  • Deep cleaning & V-groove crack routing
  • Anti-efflorescence primer treatment
  • Multi-layer elastomeric coating / liquid membrane
  • 48-hour ponding test & service warranty

Materials Used

Dr. Fixit, Asian Paints SmartCare, or Berger HomeShield — high-build elastomeric membranes, polyurethane sealants, and crystalline waterproofing compounds that withstand extreme weather and thermal expansion.

Estimated Time

2–3 days for targeted areas like bathrooms or balconies; 4–7 days for full terrace or roof waterproofing, including mandatory drying and ponding tests.
