Service Overview

Precision Fitting, Flawless Patterns

Transform living rooms, bedrooms, and feature walls with seamless wallpaper application. Our trained installers handle pattern matching, wall leveling, precision seam joining, and bubble-free bonding for non-woven, vinyl, fabric, and custom 3D wall murals.

Starting at ₹50 / sq.ft (Labor & Adhesive)

Request a Quote for This Service

What's Included

  • Wall surface inspection & minor patching
  • Wall primer/sizer application for smooth adhesion
  • Precision drop measurement & pattern alignment
  • Bubble rolling, seam trimming, & socket cutouts
  • Old wallpaper removal (available as an add-on)

Materials Used

Heavy-duty, clear CMC starch pastes and anti-fungal adhesives (Metylan, Bartoline) compatible with vinyl, non-woven, grasscloth, textured, and photo-mural rolls.

Estimated Time

3–6 hours per standard accent wall (10×12 ft); 1–2 days for a complete apartment feature wall project.
