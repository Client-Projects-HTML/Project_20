Service Overview

Protect Your Home with Long-Lasting Exterior Finishes

Our exterior painting service enhances curb appeal while protecting your property from harsh sunlight, rain, moisture, and changing weather conditions. We prepare every surface thoroughly, repair minor cracks, apply weather-resistant primers, and finish with premium exterior paints that deliver exceptional durability and vibrant colour retention.

Whether it's an individual home, apartment building, villa, office, or commercial property, our experienced painters ensure a smooth, uniform finish that withstands years of outdoor exposure.

What's Included

  • High-pressure surface cleaning
  • Crack filling and exterior wall repairs
  • Weatherproof primer application
  • Two coats of premium exterior paint
  • Final inspection and complete site cleanup

Materials Used

We use premium weather-resistant exterior paints from trusted brands such as Asian Paints Apex Ultima, Berger WeatherCoat, Dulux Weathershield, and Nerolac Excel. These coatings offer superior UV protection, resistance to heavy rain, algae prevention, and long-lasting colour retention.

Estimated Time

Most residential exterior painting projects are completed within 4–7 days, depending on building size, weather conditions, wall preparation requirements, and drying time between coats.
