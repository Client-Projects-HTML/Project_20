Who We Are

15 Years of Color, Craft, and Care

ColorCraft started as a two-person painting crew and grew into a full-service finishing company because we never stopped treating each home like our own. Every job starts with a real conversation about how you use the space — not just what color you want.

Our mission is simple: finish every wall like it's the last one we'll ever paint. Our vision is to become the most trusted name in residential and commercial finishing across the region.
